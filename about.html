We are not a typical construction firm. Nor are we a typical consultancy. We are the rare practice that does both — and has done so for over thirty years.

Our Story

Built on a unique foundation

KROS Limited was incorporated in 2009 by Savas Tuzunalper — a civil engineer by training and a financial consultant by profession. The firm was founded on a simple observation: that the best outcomes in property and construction happen when engineering intelligence and financial rigour sit in the same room.

Over fifteen years, we have grown from a consultancy practice into a full-service firm spanning construction, civil engineering, project management, and financial advisory — operating across Greater London and the Home Counties.

We work with developers, investors, and private clients. We also serve the Turkish-speaking community in London and international investors looking to enter the UK market.

Director

Savas Tuzunalper

BEng Civil Engineering MSc Banking & Finance 30+ Years Financial Consultancy Turkish-Speaking

Savas began his career as a civil engineer before completing a Masters in Banking and Finance — a combination that has defined everything KROS does. He understands the ground beneath a project as well as the numbers above it.

For over thirty years, he has advised developers, private investors, and institutional clients on development feasibility, project finance, and property investment. He has structured funding on residential, commercial, and mixed-use schemes across London.

As director of KROS, Savas leads the financial consultancy and project management divisions — taking personal responsibility for cost planning, feasibility analysis, and client relationships on every major instruction.

He offers all services in Turkish and is a trusted point of contact for Turkish-speaking clients and investors navigating the London market.
