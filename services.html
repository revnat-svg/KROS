Division 01

Construction &
Civil Engineering

From ground-up new builds to detailed interior fit-outs, KROS delivers construction works across residential, commercial, and civil sectors. Metin Demirci manages site delivery; Savas Tuzunalper oversees programme, cost, and client reporting.

Project
Management

Division 03

Financial
Consultancy

Savas Tuzunalper has been advising on property finance and investment for over thirty years. His combination of civil engineering and banking qualifications is unique in the market — and it shows in the quality of our financial analysis.

We produce development appraisals, feasibility studies, and investment assessments that are credible with lenders, investors, and planning authorities.

Consultancy Details

Services Include

  • Development feasibility studies
  • GDV (Gross Development Value) appraisals
  • Development finance advisory and introductions
  • Cost planning and elemental cost analysis
  • Investment due diligence
  • Management consultancy
  • S106 and CIL financial viability assessments
  • Land acquisition appraisals

Division 04

Property Investment
Advisory

London property is one of the most sought-after asset classes in the world. It is also one of the most complex. KROS guides investors — domestic and international — through the acquisition, structuring, and management of property investments in the capital.

We work with investors at every stage: from identifying the right opportunity, through due diligence and structuring, to ongoing management consultancy post-acquisition.

We are particularly active with Turkish investors seeking to enter or expand in the London market, offering a full Turkish-language advisory service.

  • Deal sourcing and off-market introductions

  • Residential and commercial portfolio analysis
  • Yield and capital growth projections
  • Financing strategy and lender introductions
  • Turkish-language investment consultancy
