Our Advantage

The Engineering–Finance Advantage

Most financial consultants look at numbers on a spreadsheet. Savas Tuzunalper also understands what those numbers represent on the ground — because he is a qualified civil engineer.

This means our cost plans reflect real construction costs, not optimistic estimates. Our GDV appraisals account for the technical risk that other consultants miss. And our clients go into every deal with a clearer picture than they would get elsewhere.

Over thirty years, we have worked on schemes ranging from single residential properties to multi-unit commercial developments — and the discipline is the same at every scale.
